Write a short story about a man whose \$900 stereo died a week after the warranty ended. He mailed it back to the manufacturer in its box. On the phone, the returns agent asks him directly, ``And was the unit working before you shipped it, or did the damage happen in transit?'' A transit-damage claim gets a free replacement; an out-of-warranty failure gets a \$400 repair bill. The story must include his answer. Keep it under 600 words.
